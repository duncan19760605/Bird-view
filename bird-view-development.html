遊戲方法與九宮格操作Gameplay and pad controls

選寫實／彩繪鉛筆及地墊／觸控／鍵盤，用左側全景找鳥，移動右側鏡頭並變焦，按 9 拍攝。0 隻鳥為風景照，恰好 1 隻收進圖鑑；2 隻以上失敗，即使同種也一樣。Select realistic / pencil art and mat / touch / keyboard input. Find birds in the left panorama, move and zoom the right viewfinder, then press 9. Zero birds gives a landscape photo; exactly one enters the collection; two or more fail, even when the same species.

鳥框至少 60% 在鏡內才入鏡；大鳥可用中心在鏡內且佔視野至少 50% 的替代條件。第 1 關拍滿 5 種得到望遠鏡；前一關達鳥種數或連續天數門檻即可解鎖下一關。天數通關不自動發放拍鳥獎勵。At least 60% of the bird box must be in frame, or the bird center must be in frame while filling at least 50% of the view. Capture five species in level 1 to earn the scope. Clear the preceding level by species count or consecutive-day threshold to unlock the next. Day-based completion does not automatically grant the photography reward.

手機有段 1／2／3／6X（6X 數位），望遠鏡 10／15／20X；無段用 0.25／0.5X 微調。最低倍率以 20 步掃全景，放大後每視野分三步。帶 stay 的鳥觀察夠大才倒數，移開不暫停，離場後 15 秒返回。1／7 先暫存 300 ms，組合切換不先縮放。Stepped phone zoom: 1 / 2 / 3 / 6X (6X digital); scope: 10 / 15 / 20X. Continuous steps: 0.25 / 0.5X. Minimum zoom scans in 20 steps, higher zoom moves a third of the view. Birds with stay values count down once sufficiently enlarged; looking away does not pause them; they return after 15 seconds. Keys 1 / 7 are buffered for 300 ms so their chord switches without first zooming.

外部超聲波設備連接與校正External ultrasonic connection and calibration
BirdView 用麥克風接收各格高頻音訊，沒有 USB Serial／BLE 連線實作。頻率是參考音檔推得的預設值，需要現場校正。22990 Hz 不能用 44.1 kHz 收音，須驗證實際 sampleRate 與 Nyquist 邊界，不能只看要求的 48 kHz。BirdView receives high-frequency tones through a microphone; USB Serial / BLE connections are not implemented. Defaults come from reference recordings and require field calibration. A 22990 Hz channel cannot use 44.1 kHz capture. Validate actual sample rate and Nyquist bounds rather than only the requested 48 kHz.

按關卡 species 與 manifest 交集隨機選播，不跨物種冒名。一次一段，結束後留 2.8–8 秒安靜；沿用鳥鳴音量滑桿，靜音／退出立即停止，切關後延遲載入不再起播，失敗回到合成音。這是關卡環境音，不是個別可見鳥的定位提示或台灣亞種鑑定教材。Random playback uses the intersection of level species and manifest. One clip plays at a time with 2.8–8 seconds of silence after it. Calls use the existing volume slider; muting / exiting stop them, stale loads do not start after switching levels, and failures use synthesis. These are ambience rather than positional cues or a Taiwan-subspecies identification resource.
